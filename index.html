    04 / About

    From coaching clients to guarding their data.

    Technology has always interested me, security especially. I have a B.S. in Exercise Science and spent time coaching before going all-in on IT and security, since it's a better fit for how I think.

    That coaching job mattered more than I expected. Handling clients' personal health information every day is what first got me paying attention to how small healthcare practices protect patient data, and that's the problem I keep coming back to. I now run Velari, where I build websites, hosting and AI call answering for local businesses and help small medical offices work out where their patient data actually goes.

    I think AI is going to change how most businesses operate, not just security teams, and I like digging into where it actually helps versus where it's overhyped. Building these projects taught me to direct several AI agents across different systems at once instead of running one at a time. I think that shift, from doing the work yourself to orchestrating it, will reshape careers over the next five to ten years, mine included.

    I'd rather the work make the case than a job title.

    I care about explaining a problem clearly, not just finding it.
